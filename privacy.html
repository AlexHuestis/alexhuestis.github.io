alex-os privacy policy

alex-os is used only by its owner, Alex Huestis, with his own Google account. No other users' data is accessed.

  • What it accesses: the owner's Gmail messages and Gmail filter settings, through the Gmail API with the owner's permission.
  • What it does: moves unwanted emails to Trash (never permanently deletes), creates filters that send chosen senders to Trash, and once a day writes short summaries of new emails and draft replies. Draft replies are saved as drafts in the owner's own Gmail account, where the owner reviews them and decides whether to send them himself. alex-os never sends any email: sending is blocked in its code.
  • Third parties: to write summaries and draft replies, the sender, subject and plain text (at most about 3,000 characters, without attachments) of new incoming emails is sent to Groq, an AI service. This is never done for: emails from banks, payment services, Poste Italiane, government and tax offices, the University of Bologna and account-security senders; emails with login or security codes; and advertising, social-network notifications and newsletters (these are sorted by fixed rules, without any AI). For private emails, only the sender and subject are shown to the owner. Summaries and short previews of the drafts are delivered to the owner via Telegram. Data is not sold, shared for advertising, or used for any other purpose.
  • Storage: login tokens and API keys are stored on the owner's computer and as encrypted secrets in a private GitHub repository. Email content is not stored by alex-os: summaries exist only in the owner's Telegram chat and drafts only in the owner's Gmail.
  • Google API data: alex-os's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: github.com/AlexHuestis
